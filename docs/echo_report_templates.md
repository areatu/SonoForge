# Echo Report Templates: Standardized Text Formulations

## Standard Text Formulations for Normal Structure and Function (ASE 2025 Guidelines)

### Cardiac Chambers

**Left Ventricle (LV) - Normal Structure and Function:**
- "The left ventricle is normal in size and systolic function."
- "LV cavity dimensions are within normal limits, indexed to body surface area."
- "Left ventricular ejection fraction is preserved ([insert value, typically >50-55%])."
- "Global longitudinal strain is within normal limits ([insert value, typically >-20%])."
- "Left ventricular wall thickness is normal."

**Left Atrium (LA) - Normal Structure and Function:**
- "The left atrium is normal in size, indexed to body surface area."
- "Left atrial volume index is within normal limits ([insert value, typically <34 ml/m²])."
- "Left atrial function is normal with appropriate reservoir, conduit, and pump phases."

**Right Ventricle (RV) - Normal Structure and Function:**
- "The right ventricle is normal in size and systolic function."
- "Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion (TAPSE) is within normal limits ([insert value, typically >17 mm])."
- "Right ventricular fractional area change (RVFAC) is within normal limits ([insert value, typically >35%])."

**Right Atrium (RA) - Normal Structure and Function:**
- "The right atrium is normal in size."
- "No significant atrial septal aneurysm or thrombus identified."

### Valves - Normal

**Aortic Valve (AoV) - Normal:**
- "The aortic valve is structurally normal with three mobile leaflets."
- "No aortic stenosis or regurgitation is present."
- "Aortic valve opening is adequate with no significant jet or gradient."

**Mitral Valve (MV) - Normal:**
- "The mitral valve has appropriate number of leaflets (typically two or three) with normal mobility."
- "No mitral regurgitation or stenosis is present."
- "Coaptation is adequate with no visible gap."

**Tricuspid Valve (TV) - Normal:**
- "The tricuspid valve is structurally normal with appropriate leaflet number."
- "No tricuspid regurgitation or stenosis is present."

**Pulmonary Valve (PV) - Normal:**
- "The pulmonary valve is structurally normal."
- "No pulmonary stenosis or regurgitation is present."

### Great Vessels - Normal

**Aorta:**
- "The aorta is normal in size and configuration for age and body surface area."
- "No aortic dilatation or aneurysm is identified."

**Pulmonary Artery:**
- "The pulmonary artery is normal in size."
- "No pulmonary hypertension is suggested by main pulmonary artery systolic pressure within normal limits."

### Pericardium - Normal

- "The pericardium is intact without effusion or abnormal thickening."
- "No pericardial fluid or mass is identified."

## Pathology Formulations and Gradations

### Aortic Stenosis Severity (BSE 2021 / ASE Guidelines)

| Severity | AV Vmax (m/s) | Mean Gradient (mmHg) | AVA (cm²) | Reporting Template |
|----------|---------------|----------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Aortic sclerosis** | <2.5 | – | – | "Aortic sclerosis is present with thickened valve leaflets without significant obstruction." |
| **Mild AS** | 2.5–2.9 | <20 | >1.5 | "Mild aortic stenosis is present. Aortic valve maximal velocity is [value] m/s with mean gradient of [value] mmHg and aortic valve area of [value] cm²." |
| **Moderate AS** | 3–3.9 | 20–39 | 1–1.5 | "Moderate aortic stenosis is present. Aortic valve maximal velocity is [value] m/s with mean gradient of [value] mmHg and aortic valve area of [value] cm². Hemodynamic significance should be correlated with symptoms." |
| **Severe AS** | 4–4.9 | 40–59 | <1 | "Severe aortic stenosis is present. Aortic valve maximal velocity is [value] m/s with mean gradient of [value] mmHg and aortic valve area of [value] cm². Heart team discussion for intervention evaluation recommended." |
| **Very severe AS** | ≥5 | ≥60 | ≤0.6 | "Very severe aortic stenosis is present. Aortic valve maximal velocity is [value] m/s with mean gradient of [value] mmHg and aortic valve area of [value] cm². Immediate cardiology/surgical assessment indicated." |

**Special phrases (ASE 2025):**
- "AoV gradients are increased which may be related to high stroke volume."
- "There is [mild/moderate/severe/very severe] aortic stenosis with aortic valve maximal velocity of [value] m/s and mean gradient of [value] mmHg."
- "Aortic valve area is [value] cm² ([mild/moderate/severe/very severe] AS by AVA criteria)."

### Aortic Regurgitation Severity (BSE 2021)

- **Trace/mild AR:** "Trace or mild paravalvular regurgitation is present. Jet is small with minimal LA opacification."
- **Moderate AR:** "Moderate aortic regurgitation is present. Regurgitant volume is [45-59 mL] or regurgitant fraction is [30-49%]. Jet fills approximately [50%] of LA."
- **Severe AR:** "Severe aortic regurgitation is present. Regurgitant volume is ≥60 mL or regurgitant fraction is ≥50%. Effective regurgitant orifice area is [≥0.6 cm²]. Pulmonary vein systolic flow reversal is present."

**ASE recommended phrasing:**
- "Mild paravalvular regurgitation"
- "Moderate aortic regurgitation with regurgitant volume of [value] mL and regurgitant fraction of [value]%"
- "Severe aortic regurgitation. Large jet with central jet pattern. Coaptation gap visible."

### Mitral Regurgitation Severity (BSE 2021 / ASE Guidelines)

| Criteria | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|----------|------|----------|--------|
| **Regurgitant volume** | <45 mL | 45–59 mL | ≥60 mL |
| **Regurgitant fraction** | <30% | 30–49% | ≥50% |
| **Effective regurgitant orifice area (EROA)** | <0.2 cm² | 0.2–0.39 cm² | ≥0.4 cm² (may be lower in secondary MR or when EROA is elliptic) |
| **PISA radius (at Nyquist 30–40 cm/s)** | <1.0 cm | – | ≥1.0 cm |
| **Central jet >50% of LA area** | No | No | Yes |
| **Flail leaflet** | Absent | – | Present (with complete coaptation gap) |

**Reporting templates:**
- "Mild mitral regurgitation is present. Regurgitant volume is [value] mL with regurgitant fraction of [value]%."
- "Moderate mitral regurgitation is present. Regurgitant volume is [value] mL with regurgitant fraction of [value]%. EROA is [value] cm²."
- "Severe mitral regurgitation is present. Regurgitant volume is [value] mL with regurgitant fraction of [value]%. Flail [leaflet] leaflet is present with [visible] coaptation gap. Pulmonary vein systolic flow reversal is present."

### Mitral Stenosis Severity (BSE 2021)

- **Mild MS:** MVA ≥0.4 cm² (may be lower in secondary MR or when EROA is elliptic)
- **Moderate MS:** MVA 0.2–0.39 cm²
- **Severe MS:** MVA <0.2 cm²

**Reporting template:**
- "Mitral stenosis is [mild/moderate/severe]. Mitral valve area is [value] cm²."

### Left Ventricular Systolic Dysfunction

| Severity | LVEF (%) | Reporting Template |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Normal** | ≥50% (or >55% by some guidelines) | "Left ventricular systolic function is preserved with LVEF of [value]%."
| ** mild dysfunction** | 41–49% | "There is mild LV systolic dysfunction with LVEF of [value]%."
| **Moderate dysfunction** | 30–40% | "There is moderate LV systolic dysfunction with LVEF of [value]%."
| **Severe dysfunction** | <30% | "There is severe LV systolic dysfunction with LVEF of [value]%. Global longitudinal strain is [value]%, consistent with the degree of systolic dysfunction."

**ASE recommended phrasing:**
- "There is moderate LV systolic dysfunction with an LVEF of 35%."
- "Global longitudinal strain is [value]%, indicating [normal/mild/moderate/severe] systolic dysfunction."

### Left Ventricular Diastolic Dysfunction (BSE 2024 / ASE Classification)

The ASE diastolic function classification uses an algorithmic approach incorporating multiple parameters. Key gradations:

**1. Impaired relaxation (Grade I) - "pseudonormal" physiology:**
- "Impaired relaxation with normal left ventricular compliance."
- "Prolonged isovolumetric relaxation time, reduced early diastolic filling velocities."
- "Minimum diastolic pressure is normal; LAP remains normal."
- Template: "Grade I diastolic dysfunction: impaired relaxation with normal LV compliance. Prolonged IVRT with reduced early transmitral flow velocities. Minimum filling pressure is normal."

**2. Pseudonormal filling (Grade II):**
- "Pseudonormal filling pattern with elevated LAP."
- Template: "Grade II diastolic dysfunction: pseudonormal filling pattern. Elevated left atrial pressure with restrictive filling pattern on Doppler. E wave deceleration time is short (<150 ms)."

**3. Restrictive filling (Grade III):**
- "Restrictive filling pattern with severely elevated LAP/EDP."
- Template: "Grade III diastolic dysfunction: restrictive filling pattern. Severely elevated left ventricular diastolic pressure and left atrial pressure. Short E wave deceleration time (<150 ms). Atrial contraction contributes significantly to ventricular filling."

**Simplified reporting template:**
- "Diastolic function is [normal/impaired relaxation/pseudonormal/restrictive]. [List key Doppler findings: E/A ratio, E velocity, E'/e', E deceleration time, left atrial volume index, transmitral gradient as applicable]."

### Right Heart Pressure Gradients / Pulmonary Hypertension

| Parameter | Normal | Mild PH | Moderate PH | Severe PH |
|-----------|--------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **PASP (mmHg)** | ≤35 | 36–44 | 45–54 | ≥55 |
| **TR velocity (m/s)** | <2.8 | 2.8–3.4 | 3.5–4.4 | ≥4.5 |

**Reporting template:**
- "Pulmonary artery systolic pressure is [value] mmHg (derived from peak tricuspid regurgitation velocity of [value] m/s), consistent with [normal/mild/moderate/severe] pulmonary hypertension."
- "Right ventricular systolic pressure is [value] mmHg. No significant pulmonary hypertension is present."

### Chamber Enlargement/Abnormalities

**Left Atrial Enlargement:**
- "Left atrium is mildly dilated with LA volume index of [value] ml/m² (upper normal limit [value] ml/m²)."
- "Left atrium is moderately dilated with LA volume index of [value] ml/m²."
- "Left atrium is severely dilated with LA volume index of [value] ml/m²."

**Right Atrial Enlargement:**
- "Right atrium is mildly dilated."
- "Right atrium is moderately to severely dilated."

**Left Ventricular Dilatation:**
- "Left ventricle is dilated with end-diastolic dimension of [value] cm (indexed [value] cm/m², upper normal [value] cm/m²)."
- "Left ventricle is moderately dilated."
- "Left ventricle is severely dilated."

**Right Ventricular Enlargement:**
- "Right ventricle is dilated with basal dimension of [value] cm."
- "Right ventricular volume and function are [preserved/reduced]."

### Valve Morphology Descriptions

**Normal valve descriptors (ASE 2025):**
- "The aortic valve has three symmetric leaflets with normal mobility."
- "The mitral valve has appropriate number of leaflets with adequate coaptation."
- "The tricuspid valve is trileafet with normal mobility."

**Abnormal valve descriptors:**
- "Bicuspid aortic valve with [ raphe / asymmetric calcification ]."
- "Calcific aortic valve disease with significant leaflet thickening and restricted mobility."
- "Mitral valve prolapse with [ medial / lateral / bileaflet ] billowing >2 mm past the annular plane."
- "Mitral annular calcification is present."
- "Tricuspid valve [flail / cleft / Eustachian valve / Chiari network] is present."

### Standard "Negative" / Normative Statements

**ASE 2025 recommended phrasing for normal findings:**
- "There is no significant aortic stenosis or regurgitation."
- "The aortic valve is normal in structure. There is no aortic stenosis or regurgitation."
- "No mitral regurgitation is detected."
- "Tricuspid regurgitation is trivial/mild and may be within normal limits."
- "No pulmonary stenosis or regurgitation is present."
- "The pericardium is normal without effusion."
- "No intracardiac mass, thrombus, or vegetation is identified."
- "Interatrial septum is intact without shunt."
- "Pulmonary veins demonstrate normal forward flow without reversal."

## Template Summary for Sonoforge Report Section

### Header Structure (recommended order per ASE 2025):

1. **Demographics:** Age, gender, height, weight, BSA
2. **Indication for echocardiography**
3. **Structural assessment:**
   - [Cardiac chamber sizes with indexing]
   - [Valve morphology and number of leaflets]
   - [Great vessels assessment]
   - [Pericardial assessment]
4. **Functional assessment:**
   - LV systolic function (LVEF, GLS)
   - LV diastolic function classification
   - RV systolic function
   - LA and RA function
5. **Hemodynamic assessment:**
   - Graded valvular lesions (AS, AR, MS, MR)
   - Pulmonary pressures
   - Shunt assessment (if applicable)
6. **Summary statement:**
   - Integrated findings with clinical correlation
   - Comparison with prior studies (if available)
   - Recommendations for follow-up or intervention
